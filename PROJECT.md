# Project: Guerreros de la LUZ — Complete Asteria (`https://asteriagame.com/`) Integration

## Architecture
- **Frontend & Standalone Engine (`public/` mirrored 1:1 to repository root `/`)**:
  - Zero-dependency modular vanilla HTML5/CSS3/ES6 browser MMORPG engine running identically on GitHub Pages (`index.html`, `css/`, `js/`, `assets/`) and via the local Node HTTP server (`server/index.js` serving `public/`).
  - **`public/index.html` ↔ `index.html`**: Master 3-row Gothic/Faeo shell (`header.asteria-topbar`, `main.asteria-workspace`, `footer.asteria-bottom-dock`, plus `#gdl-modal-backdrop`).
  - **`public/css/asteria.css` ↔ `css/asteria.css`**: Ornate dark-gold/bronze Faeo & Asteria theme, vitals & Valor (`Доблесть`) bar, 33.33%/66.66% XP milestone tick markers, multi-currency header badges, 12-slot paperdoll grid, 6-category backpack filter tabs, durability bars, combat stance/belt UI, hunting radar/filter overlay, and multi-system modal cards.
  - **`public/js/artAssets.js` ↔ `js/artAssets.js`**: Static database (`window.GDL_ART`) holding Factions (`Ogriy` vs `Khair`), 3 Combat Styles (`Titán`, `Destructor`, `Sombra`), 7 HD Locations, 12+ NPCs (including `pescador_neron`), 27+ Creatures (including `inquisidor_reliquia`, `serafin_piedra`, `mirmidon_piros`), 80+ Artifacts across 12 equipment slots + Runes + Scrolls + Locked Chests + Tools + Reagents, 6 directional Super-Blows, 4 Magic School Spells, 9 Professions + Crafting Recipes, 10+ Faction Reputations (including `Reputación de Tahúr / Катала` & `Influencia de Arkona`), 6 Battlefields (`Cuevas Cristalinas`, `Cuevas del Temblor`, `Salones de Tallaar`, `Batallas Caóticas`, `Meseta Antigua`, `Gran Batalla de Dragones`), 6 Instances (including Asteria's custom fast-farm **`La Gruta / ГРОТ`**), 3 Disciples Academy classes (`Ученики`), Red Pet Socket Crystals (`Красные питомцы`), 6 Collectible Card Decks (`Колоды карт`), 6 Estate Buildings (`Поместье`), 5 Clan Talents (`Клановые таланты`), Fair of Wonders / Wheel of Fortune / Daily Feats / Lucky Deposit, Promocodes (`9LETASTERII`, `DLEO-3947-URHF-3846`, `ASTERIA2026`, `SHEARA777`), and Balagur Riddles (`Конкурсы Балагуров`).
  - **`public/js/app.js` ↔ `js/app.js`**: Core state engine (`window.GDL_APP`), deep-merging `localStorage` migration (`guerreros_de_la_luz_asteria_v3`), `computeDerivedStats` (incorporating 12 slots, socketed runes, durability & `Неслом` unbreakable aura, Combat Style bonuses, profession passive bonuses, Estate/Clan Talent bonuses, Red Pet crystals, Disciples, Reputations & Medals, and injury debuffs), 33.33%/66.66% XP sub-level rewards, Anti-XP Ring (`Антиопыт`) freeze, 3-zone + stance + spell + Morok/Mount + 6-slot belt combat engine, hunting & tool-boosted harvesting, and full action handlers for every Asteria system.
  - **`public/js/uiShell.js` ↔ `js/uiShell.js`**: Top HUD renderer (HP, MP, XP with 33/66% ticks, Orange Valor bar, Multi-Currency Diamonds/Rubies/Trophy Coins, Faction badge, Anti-XP/Unbreakable/Injury status pills), Left Hero Panel (Faction & Combat Style switcher, 12-slot paperdoll with click-to-unequip & durability bar, Avatar canvas, Mount/Pet/Disciple chips, attributes), Right Dock (6-category filterable backpack without 20-slot truncation, item inspector with Equip/Unequip/Socket Rune/Repair/Assign Belt/Open Chest/Sell, Quests tab, and expanded Asteria Systems Hub).
  - **`public/js/avatarCanvas.js`, `combatCanvas.js`, `huntingCanvas.js` (mirrored to `js/`)**:
    - `avatarCanvas.js`: Renders hero paperdoll reflecting Faction (`Ogriy`/`Khair`), Combat Style (`Titán`/`Destructor`/`Sombra`), equipped weapons/shields/armor/crown, and Mount aura.
    - `combatCanvas.js`: Continuous 60fps `requestAnimationFrame` battle loop animating floating damage numbers, slash & super-blow shockwaves, active stance aura, summoned Morok/Mount & Disciple companion sprites, and combo chain pips.
    - `huntingCanvas.js`: 60fps hunting map with Monster Aggression color rings (Green/Yellow/Orange/Red), entity filter toggles (`Todos`, `Criaturas`, `Recursos`, `Jefes`), hover inspection tooltips, corner Minimap/Radar overlay, and rare Elite Guardian ambushes.
  - **`public/js/views/mainViews.js`, `specialModals.js`, `chatPanel.js` (mirrored to `js/views/`)**:
    - `mainViews.js`: Location panorama (with Collapsible Transitions & Interactive Warriors in Zone widget), Hunting mode HUD (filter pills + auto-aggro toggle + radar legend), 3-Zone Tactical Combat view (Stances, 6 Super-Blows with live step progress, Magic Spells, Morok/Mount summon button, 6-slot belt, injury banner, and post-combat `fight_info` statistics), and Regional Atlas.
    - `specialModals.js`: Complete interactive modals for NPC Dialogs (fixing `forge_shop` and `alchemy_shop`), Eltic Cube, 9 Professions & Crafting Workshop (`Taller de Artesanía`), Mounts/Red Pets/Disciples Academy (`Ученики & Красные питомцы`), Reputations & Medals (`10+ Reputaciones, Arkona & Tahúr/Катала`), Magic Mirror, Tavern Dice (with Triple Solar relic reward & Katala reputation) + Arcomage Tavern Cards, Battlefields (`Поля битв` queue & Crystalline Caves / Tallaar / Plateau objective gameplay) & Clan Talents (`Клановые таланты`), Instances & Fast-Farm Grotto (`Инстансы и ГРОТ`), Player Estate (`Hacienda / Поместье`), Collectible Card Decks (`Колоды карт`), Fair of Wonders / Wheel of Fortune / Bank Lucky Deposit / Auction House (`Ярмарка, Колесо фортуны, Банк, Аукцион`), Character Profile (`user_info.php` with Promocode redemption, Gifts & Balagur Riddles), Quests Codex, Save Manager, and Victory Cinematic.

## Interface Contracts
### `window.GDL_ART` (`public/js/artAssets.js` ↔ `js/artAssets.js`) ↔ `window.GDL_APP` (`public/js/app.js` ↔ `js/app.js`)
- `GDL_ART` exposes immutable data catalogs: `LOCATIONS`, `NPCS`, `CREATURES`, `ARTIFACTS` (with `slot`, `category`, `stats`, `maxDurability`, `priceCopper`, `minLevel`), `FACTIONS` (`ogriy`, `khair`), `COMBAT_STYLES` (`titan`, `destructor`, `sombra`), `COMBAT_STANCES`, `SUPER_BLOWS` (6 combos), `MAGIC_SPELLS` (4 spells), `PROFESSIONS` (9 professions), `CRAFT_RECIPES`, `PETS`, `PET_CRYSTALS`, `MOUNTS`, `DISCIPLES`, `REPUTATIONS` (10+ tracks), `MEDALS`, `BATTLEFIELDS` (6 modes), `INSTANCES` (6 dungeons including `gruta_grot`), `CARD_DECKS` (6 decks), `ESTATE_BUILDINGS` (6 buildings), `CLAN_TALENTS` (5 talents), `RUNES`, `DAILY_FEATS`, `FAIR_ITEMS`, `AUCTION_LISTINGS`, `PROMOCODES`, `BALAGUR_RIDDLES`, `AI_WARRIORS`, `QUESTS`, `CUBE_RECIPES`.
- `GDL_APP` exposes `getState()`, `computeDerivedStats()`, `renderAll()`, and `actions.*` covering all user interactions without throwing on legacy `localStorage` states.

### `window.GDL_UI` / `window.GDL_MAIN_VIEWS` / `window.GDL_SPECIAL_MODALS` / `window.GDL_CHAT` ↔ Canvas Engines
- `GDL_UI.renderTopHeader()`, `renderHeroLeftPanel()`, `renderRightDock()` consume `GDL_APP.getState()` and `GDL_APP.computeDerivedStats()`.
- `GDL_COMBAT_CANVAS` runs an active `requestAnimationFrame` loop when `state.ui.activeView === 'combat'` and cleanly cancels it when leaving combat view.
- `GDL_HUNTING_CANVAS` supports filter state (`state.ui.huntingFilter`: `'all' | 'creatures' | 'resources' | 'bosses'`), auto-aggro toggle (`state.ui.autoAggro`), hover tooltips, aggression color rings, and corner radar rendering.
